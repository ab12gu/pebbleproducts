Service & Retail

  • 868 service jobs booked over the last 9 months through the shop's scheduling platform. Tune-ups account for about 40% of jobs, quick fixes and minor repairs another 30%, with major repairs, custom projects, and misc labor making up the rest.
  • Service drives retail. Most parts and accessory purchases happen alongside a service visit. A customer brings in a bike for a tune and leaves with a new chain, tires, lube, or saddle. The two revenue streams are deeply linked.
  • Online retail ships nationally via Shopify with free shipping over $150. Complete bikes from Orbea, Omnium, Surly, Salsa, and the house Olallie line.
